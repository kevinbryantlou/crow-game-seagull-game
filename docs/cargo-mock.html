Each of these is the actual level, built with a different cargo list and photographed through the game's own camera from two positions — the spawn on the starboard walkway, and in among the boxes. The numbers underneath are measured off the built world, not off the table that made it. Deck hidden from camera is walkable steel the player cannot see (level 1 is 34.7%). Guards blind must never rise as the crow climbs, or flight becomes a way to hide — the one thing the sightline change must not do.
